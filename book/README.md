# The Architecture of Meaning: Persuasion, Archetypes, and Design Systems

A foundational guide exploring how visual language, cognitive psychology, and brand storytelling converge in digital design and AI-assisted workflows.

---

## About This Guide

This mini textbook examines the mechanics of human attention, identity, and aesthetics. Through historical analysis and recurring case studies—such as the multi-faceted positioning of an ordinary white T-shirt—it demonstrates how physical objects become cultural symbols. Finally, it outlines modern engineering frameworks for directing generative AI systems with specification, version control, and human oversight.
